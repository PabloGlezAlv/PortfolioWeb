PortfolioGame - Hitman-Style Stealth Prototype

  • Tech Stack: Unreal Engine 5, Blueprints, StateTree AI, Materials & Shaders, C++
  • Genre: 3D Stealth / Infiltration & Action
  • Platform: Windows PC
  • Repository: github.com/PabloGlezAlv/PortfolioGame

PortfolioGame is a third-person stealth game built in Unreal Engine 5, heavily inspired by the strategic infiltration mechanics of the Hitman series. The game transforms the traditional portfolio into a playable, interactive museum experience: every exhibit displayed along the galleries showcases real past projects developed by the creator.

🎯 Main Objective: Retrieve the CV & Escape

The player infiltrates the art museum with a clear mission: navigate the protected galleries, locate and retrieve the developer's curriculum (CV), and safely extract by returning back to the starting exit point without getting eliminated.

🧠 StateTree Enemy AI

Enemy museum guards are driven by Unreal Engine 5's modern StateTree architecture (ST_MuseumGuard). The guards perform dynamic waypoint patrols, monitor their surroundings with sight cones, investigate suspicious disturbances, enter alert states, and coordinate relentless pursuits when the intruder is spotted.

👻 Invisibility & Camouflage Mechanics

To slip past vigilant guards and navigate tight corridors, the player possesses an advanced cloaking ability. Activating invisibility alters the character's dynamic material into an electric stealth cloak, masking them from direct line of sight and granting critical moments to bypass patrol routes.

📹 Handheld Surveillance Device (Camera Book)

Equipped with a tactical handheld surveillance tablet/book, the player can inspect live feeds from closed-circuit security cameras positioned across the entire gallery. This provides essential situational awareness, allowing players to scout guard patrol patterns, spot obstacles, and plan their infiltration path beforehand.

🎨 Interactive Art Gallery of Past Projects

As players advance through the museum's interconnected wings, the gallery walls feature framed showcases and artwork representing previous works and prototypes (such as Space Scape, RTBEngine, and others), blending portfolio presentation directly into world design.
